1. Overview 
 
1.1 
Purpose and Objective 
The Data Governance Policy (the policy) establishes a framework for effective data 
management within NBQ by:  
1.1.1 establishing the principles and practices for collection, classification, protection, usage 
and sharing, transfer and destruction of personal as well as corporate data of the bank. 
1.1.2 ensuring that adequate structure and process is in place to protect the confidentiality, 
integrity and availability of bank’s data. 
1.1.3 ensuring lawful usage of data. 
1.1.4 establishing adequate roles and responsibilities for overall data governance which shall 
cater to regulatory requirements and other data law as applicable. 
1.1.5 developing a data conscious environment to provide secure, well managed and reliable 
data that supports decision-making, planning and reporting. 
1.1.6 training and awareness campaigns. 
1.1.7 compliance to the regulations. 
 
1.2 
Departments Involved / Scope 
This policy applies to all staff and affiliates including contracted parties (hereafter users) as 
well as any person with access to NBQ’s information, consumer data, and information assets.

1.4 
Key Information / Definitions / Introductions 
1.4.1 Data – Collection of facts. 
1.4.2 Consumer Data – Any personally identifiable / contextually connected data received 
from the consumer or employees which is relevant for availing or managing products 
or services from the bank. 
1.4.3 Corporate Data – All data generated or sourced for NBQ’s business purposes. 
1.4.4 Personally identifiable information (PII) - is information that, when used alone or with 
other relevant data, can identify an individual. 
1.4.5 Data Protection – Protect data from unauthorized access, use, and disclosure in 
addition to protecting it from disruption, modification, or destruction.  
1.4.6 Data Privacy - Data privacy is an area of data protection that involves proper handling 
of sensitive data, including personal data and other confidential data, such as specific 
financial data and intellectual property data, to meet regulatory requirements and 
protect the confidentiality and integrity of the data.

1.4.7 Data Quality – completeness and suitability of data to serve the specific purpose. 
1.4.8 Data Development Processes – end to end collection, processing, and storage of data.  
1.4.9 Data Environments – Collection of computer systems and associated infrastructure, 
devices, facilities, and people that support the storage, processing, or transmission of 
data supporting NBQ mission and business. 
1.4.10 Information - Structured and organized data which is meaningful or actionable.  
1.4.11 Information assets - All data, application programs and source code stored in the 
servers, in the classified personal computers / laptops and also in the form of printed 
/written materials are considered as information assets.  
1.4.12 Confidentiality -  The property that the information is not made available or disclosed to 
unauthorized individuals / entities / processes. 
1.4.13 Integrity - The property that maintains Information in a correct state so that it may not 
be tampered with, and should be correct, authentic, and reliable. 
1.4.14 Availability -The property of information being accessible and usable upon demand by 
an authorized entity. 
1.4.15 Data management control framework – Policies, standards, set of rules and controls to 
manage the life cycle of bank’s data. 
1.4.16 Privacy Impact Assessments (PIA) - Analysis of how personally identifiable information 
is collected, used, shared, and maintained. 
 
 
1.5 
Governing Laws & Regulations 
NBQ shall comply with laws of UAE and CBUAE guidelines in terms of Data Governance and 
Privacy.

1.7 
Policy Statements 
 
Data Collection 
 
1.7.1 Consumer data shall be collected only for a lawful purpose directly related to the product and 
service offered to the customer.  
1.7.2 Data collection shall be adequate to meet the purpose but not exhaustive.  
1.7.3 Annually review the data management process in the bank and address any issues Identified. 
1.7.4 Regularly carry out on the legitimacy of Data collection, access to Data, Data Integrity and 
the electronic procedures. 
 
Data Classification 
 
1.7.5 Data shall be classified as per its sensitivity. (Reference: ISMS Standard 25 – Information 
Classification Standard) 
 
1.7.5.1 Public – Information that requires no special protection or rules of use. This 
information is suitable for public dissemination. Examples include press releases, 
marketing brochures, etc.  
1.7.5.2 Internal – Information that is related to NBQ business operations, but not available 
for public consumption. This information shall only be disclosed to third parties if a 
confidentiality agreement has been signed, provided that all oral and verbal 
disclosures are always supported by written confirmation (i.e., Minutes of Meeting). 
Disclosure is not expected to cause serious harm to NBQ, and access is provided 
to all employees based on their job roles. Examples include policies and 
standards, operational procedures, etc.

1.7.5.3 Confidential – Information requiring a very high degree of protection. Access to 
this information shall be strictly restricted based on the concept of need-to-know. 
Disclosure requires the approval of the authorized custodian and/or higher 
management. In the case of third parties, a confidentiality agreement shall be 
signed, provided that all oral and verbal disclosures are always supported by 
written confirmation. If this information is compromised, there could be serious 
negative financial, operational, legal, or public image impact to NBQ or its 
members. Examples include configuration information, software code, strategy 
documents, financial documents, employee salaries, performance reviews, 
unpublished news material, etc.  
1.7.5.4 Restricted – Highly sensitive (secret) internal/external information that could 
seriously damage NBQ or other government entities if lost or made public. 
Information in this category has very restrictive distribution and shall be protected 
at all times. Security at this level is extremely high. 
Data Protection 
1.7.6 NBQ shall provide adequate protection to its customers and corporate data by complying 
with NBQ policies. 
1.7.7 Data should be collected with appropriate security and protection measures against 
unauthorized or unlawful processing and accidental loss, destruction, or damage. 
1.7.8 Adequate monitoring and preventive controls shall be in place to detect any unauthorized or 
accidental loss, misuse, modification, access, disclosure or destruction of data. Details of 
such findings need to be maintained as per the bank’s data retention policy in line with 
regulatory requirements.  
1.7.9 Incident Management Standard of the bank shall be followed in case of any data breach. 
Reference: ISMS Standard 15 – Incident management & Reporting Standard 
 
Data Usage  
1.7.10 Usage of consumer data shall be lawful. NBQ shall declare its intent to use and/or share 
consumers’ personally identifiable information. NBQ shall do so by obtaining expressed 
consent from the consumers. 
1.7.11 NBQ shall ensure that consumers are able to make informed choices with respect to 
providing expressed consent as to their data being collected, used, and shared with third 
parties and within the bank.  
1.7.12 Adequate contractual and non-disclosure agreements with all third parties shall be in place.  
1.7.13 NBQ shall comply with regulatory requirements and applicable data laws related to the data 
usage. 
 
Data Destruction 
 
 
1.7.14 All data, documents, records, and files shall be securely retained as per the regulatory 
requirements. The bank shall also keep electronic data in its transaction processing and other 
supporting systems to meet business and legal requirements.  
1.7.15 All agreements with the consumers related to confidentiality and security shall be maintained 
after the termination of the relationship until the Consumer Data is destroyed. 
1.7.16 Expressed consents collected from the customers for usage and sharing of data need to be 
retained as per the regulatory requirements after the relationship with the Consumer has 
terminated.

1.7.17 Detailed monitoring of records and the actions taken during Privacy Impact Assessments and 
other data protection related processes shall be maintained as per the regulatory

1.7.18 NBQ shall not process or use Consumer Data for any period longer than is necessary for the

mandatory retention period for retaining Consumer records, NBQ shall take all reasonable 
steps to ensure that all Consumer Data is destroyed or permanently deleted or securely

and processed or no longer required by law.
